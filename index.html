Clear Facts

Frequently Asked Questions

What gives Winkin' Cow Rose its authentic aroma? expand_more

We infuse distilled natural Damask rose essence paired with creamy double-toned whole dairy milk. There are no harsh chemical synthetic fragrances.

How long does an unopened bottle stay fresh? expand_more

Thanks to our state-of-the-art Aseptic Ultra High Temperature (UHT) packaging, unopened bottles remain fresh for up to 6 months at room temperature. Once opened, refrigerate and consume within 24 hours.

Is it 100% vegetarian and kid-friendly? expand_more

Yes! It features the green vegetarian mark and is packed with 8.5g pure dairy protein, 35% calcium, and Vitamin D3—making it a healthy, joyful drink for school kids and adults alike.

Should I shake it before sipping? expand_more

Absolutely! Give it a hearty 5-second shake while chilled to release micro-bubbles that turn the texture exceptionally velvety.
